Rudolph’s Retro Trading is a three-page website for a small business that buys and sells collectable items such as books, toys, antiques and technology. The purpose of the site is to present the collection clearly and let visitors explore it interactively. It is built with HTML for structure, one shared CSS file for presentation, and JavaScript that changes the page through the DOM. The intended target market is collectors and casual browsers who have a passion for collecting things, regardless of value and condition, who want to see what is available and judge each item’s condition. Each item has a score from 1 to 10, where 1 is terrible condition and 10 is the best.
